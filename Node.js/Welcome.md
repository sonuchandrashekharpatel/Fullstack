# Node.js

## Developed in 2009, Ryan Dahl had an Idea.

## Where you find Node.js
1. Server-rendered apps
2. Microservices
3. Backend Services for mobile
4. Bots and Scrappers
5. Desktop apps


## Node.js is NOT a language.

## Requirements
1. A solid foundation in JavaScript
    . map, reduce, filter
    . async/await

2. No frameworks needed
3. No knowledge of Node.js or and other backend technology needed

## Why it's good to learn Node.js

1. Get to really understand Node.js
2. Makes learning frameworks easier
    . NestJS, fastify
3. Node.js is used in production apps without frameworks
